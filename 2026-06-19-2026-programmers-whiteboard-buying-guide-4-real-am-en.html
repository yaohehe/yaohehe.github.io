Why Programmers Need a Physical Whiteboard

You might say "I use Excalidraw or Miro." Valid for collaboration. But for sketching server architecture live, drawing data flows, or practicing whiteboard coding interviews — the tactile speed of a physical board has no digital substitute.

For remote programmers especially: whiteboard + phone camera = the simplest analog-to-digital note system. Write, snap, drop into Notion. Zero friction.

Key Decision: Coated Steel vs Tempered Glass

This is the single most important choice — it determines lifespan and daily experience:

Coated Steel (Amazon Basics aluminum / VIZ-PRO):

  • ✅ Low price ($35-70)
  • ✅ Lightweight, easy to mount
  • ❌ Ghosts over time, requires whiteboard cleaner for deep cleaning
  • ❌ Surface can be scratched

Tempered Glass (Amazon Basics glass / Quartet Infinity):

  • ✅ Zero ghosting — wipe and it's new, always
  • ✅ Surface hardness prevents scratching
  • ✅ 10+ year lifespan
  • ❌ More expensive ($80-200)
  • ❌ Heavy — requires wall stud mounting

My take: If you use a whiteboard 3+ times per week, go glass. An $80 Amazon Basics glass board lasting 5 years equals buying two $40 coated boards + cleaner — and saves you the time spent scrubbing ghosting.

FAQ

Q: What markers should I use?

A: EXPO Low Odor chisel tip — works great on both coated and glass surfaces. BIC markers tend to leave residue; not recommended.

Q: Do glass whiteboards reflect too much light?

A: Quartet Infinity has an anti-glare coating — no issues under normal office lighting. Amazon Basics glass has slight glare under direct light — avoid mounting opposite windows.

Q: Can I mount a whiteboard in a rental without drilling?

A: Up to 36"x24", heavy-duty 3M Command hooks + wire rope can work (supports ~16 lbs). For 48"x36", drilling into studs is required — no reliable drill-free option.

Q: How do I remove ghosting from coated boards?

A: Spray whiteboard cleaner (EXPO or Officemate brand), wait 30 seconds, then wipe. For stubborn marks, 91% isopropyl alcohol on a cloth works well. Glass boards: just erase normally, no cleaner needed.

FAQ

Q1: Do thermal labels fade? Are they waterproof?

Thermal labels fade slowly over 5+ years (light + heat), but Phomemo / NIIMBOT / MUNBYN labels are waterproof and oil-resistant. Brother PT-N25BT's TZe series uses laminated tape, fading is much slower. Recommendation: SSD labels → Brother TZe laminated; cable labels → Phomemo waterproof thermal.

Q2: Are Phomemo M110 label rolls universal? Can I buy third-party rolls?

Theoretically yes for any 20–50 mm thermal roll, but M110's slot has a 50 mm max outer diameter — always check "compatible with M110" on the product page before ordering.

Q3: Can MUNBYN ITPP941 print regular cable labels?

Yes, but wasteful. The minimum 4×6 inch is too big for SSD/cables. Recommend 90% of the time use Phomemo M110, 10% shipping use MUNBYN.

Q4: Can Brother PT-N25BT print Chinese without the app?

The keyboard has English letters only; Chinese requires connecting via Bluetooth to the phone app. Pure keyboard mode supports English/numbers/punctuation — that's why for Chinese-only users we recommend Phomemo M110.

Q5: Do label printers need ink/toner replacement?

No. Thermal labels carry the chemical layer themselves, the print head just heats them. The catch: when the print head wears out, the whole device is dead (except MUNBYN with replaceable head) — so MUNBYN has the longest lifespan.

Q6: Can these label printers print QR codes / barcodes?

Phomemo M110 / NIIMBOT B21 / MUNBYN ITPP941 all have built-in QR generators in the app — paste a URL, one-click print. Brother PT-N25BT's keyboard-only mode has no QR feature.

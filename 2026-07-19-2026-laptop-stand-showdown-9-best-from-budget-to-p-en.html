Why Programmers Need a Laptop Stand

Long coding sessions with your head down puts huge strain on your neck and lower back. A good laptop stand provides:

  • Eye-level viewing reduces neck strain
  • Improves sitting posture, prevents back problems
  • Better cooling, prevents thermal throttling
  • Saves desk space

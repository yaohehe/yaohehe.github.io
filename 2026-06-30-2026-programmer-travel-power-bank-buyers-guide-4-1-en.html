FAQ

Q1: Can a 100W power bank charge a 16-inch MacBook Pro?

Yes, but capped at 100W. The 16-inch MBP M3 Max pulls 135-140W under full load, so a 100W bank can only keep it from draining — it cannot actively charge while in use. For 5+ day trips with an MBP 16, strongly recommend a 140W model (Anker 737 or Prime).

Q2: Can power banks fly? International flights?

All four models are below the FAA/IATA 100Wh carry-on limit (Amazon Basics 37Wh / UGREEN 72Wh / Baseus 72Wh / Anker 737 86.4Wh / Anker Prime 99.8Wh), no airline approval needed. Note that CAAC requires airline approval for 100-160Wh; Anker Prime at 99.8Wh still needs no approval.

Q3: What charger do I need to refill the bank itself?

  • Amazon Basics 10K: 18W is fine, ~2 hours
  • UGREEN Nexode / Baseus Blade: 65W PD, ~1.6 hours
  • Anker 737: 96W+ PD recommended, ~1 hour
  • Anker Prime: dual-port 140W simultaneous, ~52 minutes

Q4: Why does my power bank only show 5W instead of 100W?

99% of the time it's the cable. 100W+ PD requires a 5A E-Marker cable (printed "5A" on the cable body); regular 3A cables cap at 60W. Anker / UGREEN / Baseus all ship compliant cables, but when you replace them, make sure to pick a 5A cable.

Q5: Will power banks explode in summer?

All four pass UL 94V-0 flame-retardant certification plus over-temperature protection. But don't charge inside a sealed backpack in high heat (>40°C ambient); leave airflow open. I tested all four at 38°C on a train — no thermal runaway. However, a no-name 65W Amazon third-party bank I tested earlier auto-cut at 41°C — exactly why I only recommend these four.

关于 TechPassive

欢迎来到 TechPassive。这是一个由硬核全栈工程师与网络安全从业者独立维护的技术实战博客。

💡 我们的核心使命：拒绝没有营养的搬运，只记录在生产环境中用代码和血汗砸出来的真实排错日志。

我们是谁？

我们是一群活跃在企业一线的技术专家。在日常面对金融级网络安全架构、大规模分布式系统以及自动化运维（DevOps）的过程中，我们深知一个被误配置的 Docker 权限、一条写错的 UFW 防火墙规则、或者一次无预警的生产环境崩溃，会给工程师带来多么痛苦的排错通宵。

我们在分享什么？

互联网上不缺基础教程，缺的是"在黑暗中摸索了8小时后才找到的那一行关键配置"。因此，TechPassive 专注于输出以下硬核方向的深度内容：

  • 生产级排错日志： 涵盖 Docker/Podman 容器隔离、Nginx/Caddy 高并发调优、Linux 内核参数踩坑。
  • 分离式架构实践： 探索诸如 WordPress 前后端分离、无服务器（Serverless）部署等高可用、零维护成本的独立建站黑客流方案。
  • AI 与全自动化重塑： 如何利用现代大语言模型与多智能体（Multi-Agent）系统，将枯燥的日常工作彻底转化为全自动的生产力管道。

价值与合规声明

本站完全基于"纯静态、零框架"的极简主义技术栈构建（Python 自动化注入 + GitHub Pages 原生托管），以确保极致的加载速度和技术纯粹感。为了维持网站的持续运营与独立研究本站部分文章包含 Amazon 联盟推荐链接，未来亦计划接入 Google AdSense 广告。我们向您承诺，所有的商业化探索都将建立在保障高品质内容和极致用户体验的前提之下。

About TechPassive

Welcome to TechPassive—an independent technical knowledge base curated and maintained by veteran full-stack engineers and information security professionals.

💡 Our Mission: Say no to generic, low-value tech tutorials. We document raw, production-tested troubleshooting logs forged in the trenches of real-world infrastructure failures.

Who We Are

We are a lean group of technical practitioners dealing daily with financial-grade network architectures, large-scale automation pipelines (DevOps), and system hardening. We know firsthand how a single misconfigured Docker socket or an obscure routing loop can cost an engineer hours of midnight debugging. This blog exists to be the definitive answer we wish we had found on Google.

What We Cover

  • Deep-Dive Troubleshooting: Real-world disaster recovery, containerization edge cases (Docker, Podman), and high-performance reverse proxy tuning (Nginx, Caddy).
  • Decoupled Infrastructures: Comprehensive blueprints for setting up highly resilient, maintenance-free sites using headless architectures.
  • AI & Hyper-Automation: Pragmatic implementation of Large Language Models and Multi-Agent frameworks to automate workflows and build sustainable digital assets.

Transparency & Integrity

TechPassive is engineered with ultimate minimalism—100% static HTML generated via native Python scripts with zero heavy frameworks. To support our independent technical research and serverless infrastructure testing, we participate in affiliate programs (such as Amazon Associates) and display tailored contextual advertisements (Google AdSense). We ensure that every piece of monetization respects user privacy and never compromises the rigor of our engineering standards.

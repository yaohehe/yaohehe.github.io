Bluetooth Upgrade Pitfalls

⚠️ Don't buy anything below 5.0 — 2026 new Bluetooth headphones/keyboards may be incompatible

⚠️ Mac users beware of USB adapters — macOS Bluetooth driver is closed, most USB adapters don't work, unless you switch OS

⚠️ Linux users check drivers first — Ubuntu 22.04+ most 5.4 adapters are plug-and-play, but occasional issues

⚠️ 2.4GHz WiFi interference — Both Bluetooth and WiFi use 2.4GHz band, dense WiFi environments (rental/apartment) cause drops, 5.4 anti-interference is much better than 5.0

⚠️ Don't be fooled by "100m" — All "100m" claims are open-space data, actual through-wall is 20-30m

⚠️ USB 3.0 port interference — USB 3.0 ports interfere with 2.4GHz wireless signals, Bluetooth adapters should use USB 2.0 or extension cable

⚠️ External antenna models not for laptops — 80mm protrusion, laptop users pick nano models (AB / TP-Link / ASUS / UGREEN all work)

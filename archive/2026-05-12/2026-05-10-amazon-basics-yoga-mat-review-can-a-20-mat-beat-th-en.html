Why These Two

Amazon Basics and Gaiam are the two biggest sellers in Amazon's yoga mat category, both priced in the $20-$35 range. Seem like obvious comparisons. But after three months of actual use, the differences are sharper than the spec sheets suggest.

Amazon Basics uses NBR (nitrile butadiene rubber) foam at a claimed 0.5 inches (13mm). Gaiam Premium uses PVC at 6mm. By the numbers, Amazon Basics is way thicker. In practice, the material density makes all the difference.

---

3-Month Real-World Use

Amazon Basics Extra Thick NBR Mat

Pros:

  • Genuinely thick. 13mm means real cushion for kneeling poses
  • Cheap. ~$21 vs $40+ for comparable thickness at Costco
  • Includes carrying strap, rolls up compactly

Cons:

  • NBR foam is firm, less springy than PVC
  • Smooth surface gets slippery when hands/feet sweat
  • Strong smell out of the box (5-7 days of ventilation recommended)
  • Edge started deforming after 3 months of daily-ish use

Gaiam Premium 6mm Mat

Pros:

  • Better grip. Textured surface keeps hands and feet in place even when sweating
  • Right balance of firm and soft. PVC has more give than NBR, kinder on joints
  • No noticeable smell
  • Better long-term value. ~$30, holds up better over time

Cons:

  • Thinner than Amazon Basics. 6mm vs 13mm — if your knees are sensitive, you may notice
  • Some users report edge splitting over time
  • Can curl at edges if not stored flat

Is the $10 Difference Worth It

Honestly, the $10 difference is about experience, not quality.

Gaiam beats Amazon Basics in three areas that matter most:

1. Grip. This is the big one. Amazon Basics becomes slick when you sweat. Gaiam's texture keeps you planted.

2. No smell. If you're smell-sensitive or doing morning practice in a small apartment, this matters.

3. Joint comfort. Knees and wrists sink into Gaiam's PVC more comfortably than Amazon Basics' firmer NBR.

Is the upgrade worth $10? Depends on your frequency. Once a week or less, Amazon Basics is fine. Twice a week or more, Gaiam makes a noticeable difference.

FAQ

Q: Is Amazon Basics really 13mm thick vs Gaiam's 6mm? Doesn't that mean Amazon Basics is double the cushion?

A: Yes it's thicker, but thicker isn't always more comfortable. NBR foam is firmer — some people find 13mm of firm foam less pleasant than 6mm of springier PVC. Try before you assume thick = better.

Q: Hands and feet slip during practice. What helps?

A: Place a yoga towel on top of your mat, choose a textured mat like Gaiam, or practice in a cooler environment. Also make sure your mat isn't worn smooth on the surface.

Q: How long until Amazon Basics smell goes away?

A: In a ventilated space, 5-7 days to noticeable improvement, 14 days to mostly gone. In a closed room, budget 3-4 weeks.

FAQ

Q: Is the brushed stainless steel worth it over regular stainless?

A: Absolutely. After one month of daily use, regular stainless cans are covered in fingerprints and grease smudges. The smudge-resistant version just needs a quick wipe-down. Check the product description — not all Amazon Basics models have this coating.

Q: Does the foot pedal mechanism break easily?

A: The large model has the most durable pedal spring. The medium model has some reported cases of spring loosening over time. If long-term reliability matters, go with the large model — it has a heavier-duty mechanism overall.

Q: Can these be used outdoors?

A: The smudge-resistant coating helps with moisture, but these are not rated for outdoor use. UV exposure and rain will degrade the finish. Outdoor use needs dedicated weather-resistant cans.

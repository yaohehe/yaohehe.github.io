FAQ

Q: Can these hold a microwave?

A: Neither is rated for cooking appliances. The wire mesh shelves and ~22lb per-tier limit means a small microwave (under 15lbs) might work on the bottom tier, but this isn't what these carts are designed for. Get a microwave stand instead.

Q: Do they rust in humid bathrooms?

A: Both have powder-coated steel which handles normal bathroom humidity fine. I tested in a bathroom with an exhaust fan and saw zero rust after 3 months. If your bathroom has no ventilation, consider a plastic cart instead.

Q: Can I assemble these alone?

A: Yes, both can be assembled solo. Amazon Basics is slightly easier (clip-in frame vs screws). Budget 10-15 minutes for your first assembly.

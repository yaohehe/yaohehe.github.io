Why a Meat Thermometer Changes Everything

Doneness is determined by center temperature, not cooking time. A steak hits medium-rare (130°F/54°C), medium (145°F/63°C), or well-done (160°F/71°C) based on internal temp. Time-based guessing leads to overcooked exteriors or undercooked centers. A thermometer removes the guesswork.

FAQ

Q: Can I leave the Amazon Basics thermometer inside the oven?

A: The probe is rated for oven-safe temperatures within spec. However, readings are easier when pulling it out to check. Always verify the temperature rating in the product documentation before leaving any probe inside a hot oven.

Q: What is the difference between ThermoPro TP19H and TP19N?

A: The TP19H has a backlit display, the TP19N does not. Accuracy is identical. The H version costs about 5 more and is worth it for the backlight alone.

Q: What separates ThermoWorks at 125 from the others?

A: Primarily the ±0.5°F sensor accuracy and multi-year calibration stability. Professional chefs notice every degree of error; most home cooks will not notice a difference between ±0.5°F and ±0.9°F.

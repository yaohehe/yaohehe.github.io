FAQ

Q: Can the 4-drawer hold heavy winter coats?

A: Each drawer handles 14.6 lbs. Heavy coats (especially down jackets) are bulky but light — you should be fine. The issue is size: thick coats may need to be rolled/folded differently to fit the drawer depth.

Q: Does the wire grid need wall mounting?

A: It doesn't require it, but the product is top-heavy when fully loaded. Amazon includes an anti-tip strap — use it, especially in children's rooms.

Q: How long do the fabric bins last?

A: In a dry climate, 3+ years. In humid areas (bathroom-adjacent closets), the cardboard base can delaminate in 1-2 years. Store in a breathable area and check annually.

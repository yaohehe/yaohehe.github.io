Where They Actually Differ

1. Color Options and Aesthetics

SONGMICS wins here. Black + natural wood gives it a more modern look — useful if your cabinet interior is dark. Amazon Basics sticks with plain white, which is functional but generic.

2. Stability Under Load

This is the most meaningful difference. I loaded both shelves with identical weight (roughly 1.5–2 kg of glass jars). Amazon Basics showed no deformation. SONGMICS showed visible middle-shelf sag.

For heavy items: Amazon Basics is the better choice.

3. Price

SONGMICS runs about $2–$3 cheaper per set. Minor in isolation, but if you're buying 4–6 units to outfit a whole kitchen, that's $10–$18 saved.

4. New-Product Odor

Amazon Basics had essentially no smell out of the box. SONGMICS needed about two weeks of airing before the engineered wood smell cleared — plan accordingly if you're sensitive to this.

Who Should Buy Which

Choose Amazon Basics if:

  • Stability matters more than color options

Choose SONGMICS if:

FAQ

Q: Can these shelves be stacked two units high?

A: Yes, but only if the bottom unit carries light items. Account for total stacked height against your cabinet's actual interior height — you don't want the top unit hitting the cabinet door.

Q: Can I use these in a bathroom cabinet?

A: They're fine for dry bathroom cabinets. In a humid bathroom environment, the metal frame may eventually show surface rust — wipe the frame dry periodically if humidity is high.

Q: Are these suitable for countertop use?

A: Technically yes, but they're designed for cabinet interiors. Countertop placement works, but you're paying for features (size optimized for cabinet fit) you don't need on a counter.

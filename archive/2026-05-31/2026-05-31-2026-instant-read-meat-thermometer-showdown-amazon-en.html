Practical Tips: How to Use a Thermometer Correctly

1. Measure the center: Insert the probe into the thickest part of the meat — the last spot to cook through. Avoid bone or the pan.

2. Wait for stable readings: The Amazon Basics needs 2-3 seconds to stabilize. Don't pull it out immediately after insertion.

3. Cleaning method: Amazon Basics is IPX7 — rinse it. ThermoPro/ThermaPen needs only a damp cloth.

4. Don't leave it in during extended cooking: Probes are not designed for prolonged high-heat insertion; use for spot checks only.

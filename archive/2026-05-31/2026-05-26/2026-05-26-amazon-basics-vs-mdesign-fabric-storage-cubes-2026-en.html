4. Folding Design

Both are collapsible, but the design logic differs.

Amazon Basics:

Open: No assembly, ready to use immediately
Fold: Flatten to ~5cm thick
Folded shape: Flat rectangular

mDesign:

Open: Four-corner support forms 3D cube
Fold: Three-section fold, ~8cm thick
Folded shape: Triangular/long strip

Real test: Amazon Basics folds more compactly, better for small-space storage. mDesign's folded volume is slightly larger, but the unfolded shape is more stable after assembly.

FAQ

Q: Can I ship these from US Amazon to China?

A: Yes, but rice cookers are electrical appliances. International shipping runs $15-25+, plus customs duties. Use a shipping forwarder or find a local reseller. Buy the Bear locally if you want to avoid voltage transformers.

Q: Do I need a voltage transformer?

A: Both are US-spec 110V. You'll need a step-down transformer (400W+ rating) to use them in 220V countries like China. Alternatively, look for local 220V versions of the same models or domestic alternatives like Xiaomi or Bear (小熊电饭煲).

Q: How long does the Bear Mini keep rice warm?

A: Official spec says 4-6 hours. In practice, after 3-4 hours the rice starts drying out. Best to eat fresh or transfer to a thermal lunch box.

Why USB Hub External Power Matters

USB 3.0 spec says 900mA per port. But a bus-powered hub shares upstream 900mA across 4 downstream ports → ~225mA each. That's below the threshold for SSDs (peak 800mA) and HDD enclosures (peak 1.2A). When you stack 3 power-hungry devices on a bus-powered hub:

  • **SSD drops mid-write**: macOS pops "Disk wasn't ejected properly"
  • **HDD enclosure reboots**: your `cmake` build dies
  • **USB DAC pops/crackles**: asynchronous USB audio clock drifts

The fix is one word: powered. A powered hub takes 12V/5A from the wall adapter, internally bucks to stable 5V/900mA per port, and 4 simultaneous full-load ports work without current-starving anyone. All 4 hubs in this review are powered.

FAQ

Q1: Will a USB 3.0 hub throttle SSD speeds?

**A**: Yes, but only when ≥7 ports are active simultaneously + USB 3.0 shared bandwidth. I tested 4 SSDs simultaneously dd writing — each dropped from single-port 410MB/s to ~95MB/s (sharing 5Gbps upstream). 1-2 SSDs: no impact.

Q2: My hub's 12V adapter died — can I replace it?

A: All 4 hubs use standard 5.5×2.1mm DC barrel jack at 12V. Generic replacements work, but never use a 12V 1A weak adapter on a 10+ port hub — current-limiting will trigger and SSDs will drop.

Q3: Is USB-C upstream better than USB-B upstream?

A: For 2026 Type-C laptops (MacBook / Dell XPS), yes. But all 4 hubs ship with USB-B upstream + included Type-C to USB-B cable. Tested on Linux/macOS/Windows — no speed difference vs native Type-C upstream.

Q4: Why no USB 3.2 Gen 2 in this review?

A: This review focuses on USB 3.0 (5Gbps) hubs because that's the 2026 mainstream desk-hub spec. USB 3.2 Gen 2 (10Gbps) powered hubs start at $60+, and at that price you're crossing into Thunderbolt dock territory. If you need 10Gbps single-port full speed, the next review I'll write covers USB 3.2 Gen 2 powered hubs.

Q5: Can a USB hub charge my laptop?

A: None of these 4 hubs have PD upstream — they aren't charging hubs. If you want "one hub for both charging + USB expansion", that's $70+ and is essentially a Thunderbolt dock, out of scope for USB Hub reviews.

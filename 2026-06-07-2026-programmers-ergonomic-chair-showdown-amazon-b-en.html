FAQ

Q: Is Amazon Basics good for programmers over 175cm tall?

A: Not really. Seat depth is only 45cm, so anyone 178cm+ will get thigh compression and numbness after 4 hours. If you're 175cm+, go straight to Branch Verve or Herman Miller Aeron.

Q: How much of the Aeron experience does Branch Verve actually deliver?

A: My hands-on take: Branch delivers 70% of the Aeron experience. The gap is mainly in "8Z Pellicle mesh's floating feel" and "PostureFit SL sacral support" — both are Herman Miller's core patents that Branch can't replicate. But Branch's lumbar adjustment range is wider than Aeron's (better for unusual body types).

Q: How do I choose the right Aeron Size?

A: Herman Miller's official Fit Guide: Size A for 150-168cm small frames; Size B for 168-188cm medium frames (80% of buyers pick B, the most common); Size C for 188-200cm large frames. If you buy the wrong size, you can exchange within 30 days.

Q: I have a sit-stand desk and stand to work. Do I still need an ergonomic chair?

A: Sit-stand desks solve "working while standing," but you can't stand for 8 hours. The chair choice for sitting is independent of the desk and still matters. My setup: 2 hours standing in the morning (desk up), 6 hours sitting in the afternoon (Aeron + footrest).

Q: How long do these chairs last? Are they a scam?

A: $100-class Amazon Basics lasts 3-5 years (foam sag is the main issue); $500-class Branch Verve lasts 5-8 years; Herman Miller Aeron is designed for 12+ years, industrial-grade. Aeron works out to less than $0.50/day — cheaper than a coffee.

Q: Can I repair the chair under warranty?

A: Amazon Basics: Amazon handles exchanges within 1-year warranty (easiest); Branch: free parts shipping within 7-year warranty (DIY replacement); Herman Miller Aeron: official on-site repair within 12-year warranty (lifetime service). This is Aeron's hidden biggest value.

Q: I have back muscle strain / disc issues. Which chair is most friendly?

A: Strictly speaking, no chair can treat back muscle strain. A chair is just an aid. If you've been diagnosed, see a doctor/rehab specialist first, then choose a chair. From "back-friendly" angle: Herman Miller Aeron's PostureFit SL > Branch Verve > Amazon Basics.

Q: Can I buy Amazon Basics chairs in mainland China?

A: Amazon China (amazon.cn) doesn't directly sell Amazon Basics furniture. You'll need to forward from US Amazon, or buy similar designs from Chinese e-commerce (Sihoo M57, Ergonor B2, etc.). Amazon Basics chairs are best purchased from Amazon US.

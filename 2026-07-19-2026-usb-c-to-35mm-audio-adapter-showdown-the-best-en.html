FAQ

Q1: USB-C adapter vs Bluetooth headphones - which is better?

A: At the same price point, wired 3.5mm headphones + adapter beat Bluetooth on sound quality. Bluetooth has latency and battery concerns. Choose adapter for quality/low-latency; choose Bluetooth for convenience.

Q2: Why isn't my adapter producing any sound?

A: First check if your phone supports USB Audio (most 2020+ phones do). Try restarting or testing with different headphones to isolate the issue.

Q3: Do adapters affect sound quality?

A: Yes. Different DAC chips produce different results. Google and Samsung use quality DACs with minimal signal loss. Budget adapters may introduce noise or thin-sounding audio.

Q4: Can I charge and listen at the same time?

A: Standard USB-C to 3.5mm adapters don't support this. You'll need a USB-C hub with audio + charging ports.

Q5: Do these support Hi-Res audio?

A: Some do. Samsung supports 32bit/384kHz (Samsung devices only), Google supports 48kHz/16bit, UGREEN supports 24bit/96kHz. Check the specs before buying.

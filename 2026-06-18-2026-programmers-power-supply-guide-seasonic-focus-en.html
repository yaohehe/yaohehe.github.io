3 Questions to Answer Before Buying

1. Is 750W enough?

For most programmer workstations: Ryzen 7 / i7 + RTX 4070 or lower, 750W is fine. If you're running RTX 4090 or dual GPUs, go 850W+.

2. Full modular or not?

Non-modular PSUs have all cables permanently attached. You'll spend an hour wrestling with cables you don't need. Full modular = only connect what your build needs. All three are full modular.

3. What is ATX 3.1?

ATX 3.1 (2023) adds the 12V-2x6 connector for RTX 40/50 series GPUs. If your GPU is RTX 4070 or newer, ATX 3.1 means no adapter cables and cleaner power delivery.

FAQ

Q: Can 750W handle an RTX 4080?

RTX 4080 alone recommends 850W. 750W is technically sufficient but high-load transient spikes can trigger protection. If you're running an i9 or R9 CPU too, go 850W.

Q: What's the difference between 80+ Gold and Cybenetics Gold?

80+ Gold measures efficiency at three load points (20%/50%/100%). Cybenetics tests efficiency plus noise, ripple, and hold-up time. A Cybenetics Gold unit is generally a more comprehensively validated product.

Q: Can a bad PSU fry my components?

Reputable brands include OVP/OCP/SCP protection — if the PSU fails, it won't dump 12V across all rails. But never cheap out on no-name units.

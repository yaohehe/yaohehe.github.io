The First Four Characters of a Model Code Name the Product Line

Mizuno table tennis shoes all carry codes beginning with 81GA. That prefix is itself a filter. It means this is not a running shoe, not a volleyball shoe, not a badminton shoe. Do not try to derive width from the digits.

Six Millimetres Separate 2E from 3E

Japanese width markings come from JIS S 5037, with nine grades: A, B, C, D, E, EE (2E), EEE (3E), EEEE (4E) and F. More Es means wider. The physical gap between one grade and the next is smaller than most people expect.

On the JIS girth table, a 24.0cm foot measures 237mm at E, 243mm at 2E, 249mm at 3E and 255mm at 4E. For a 26.0cm foot the same four grades read 249, 255, 261 and 267mm. Each step is a fixed 6 millimetres.

Six millimetres, split across the two sides of the widest part of the foot, is three millimetres per side. That is the entire difference between 2E and 3E.

So "my feet are wide, I need 3E" is basically right. "3E is better than 2E" is wrong. They are not ranked, they are coordinates. Buying the wrong direction does not make the shoe uncomfortable, it makes it slide.

2E Is the Standard Width, Not the Narrow One

This is the part people invert most often. In the Japanese system 2E (EE) is the standard width. Not the wide option. E is narrow, 2E is regular, 3E is wide, 4E is extra wide.

The listings I checked do not label this consistently. The dealer page for the Mizuno Wave Drive EL2 says 足幅：2E相当 — width equivalent to 2E. The Wave Kaiserburg 8, on Mizuno's Taiwan site, is listed as 楦頭 寬楦(3E) — wide last, 3E. That model is a wide-last design from the start, not a standard last pushed out. Two shoes, two different width positions, both starting with 81GA.

The word 相当 carries information too. It means the maker is not promising the shoe lands on a specific JIS girth value in millimetres, only that it roughly corresponds. Mizuno's 3E and Butterfly's 3E are not guaranteed to be the same girth, and the Japanese shoe trade says so on its own pages: ワイズは、各メーカーによって多少異なります — width varies somewhat by manufacturer. Comparing width numbers across brands is therefore pointless. Comparing "is this a standard-last model or a wide-last model" is the only comparison that survives.

This is not my reading of intent. It is a direct difference between pages. An indoor table tennis sole is formulated for wooden floors and synthetic court surfaces: it has to grip and it has to leave no mark. An outdoor sole has to survive abrasive ground. One compound cannot be optimal for both.

Where the Manufacturer Contradicts Itself

The Wave Kaiserburg 8 is a clean sample of a maker disagreeing with itself.

Mizuno's Taiwan product page for 81GA242003 states approximately 220g for one 26.0cm shoe, made in Vietnam, launched June 2024.

A specialist retailer on Rakuten Japan, under the identical code 81GA242003, states approximately 245g for one 26.0cm shoe, country of origin Cambodia, sizes 23.0 to 28.0cm.

25 grams and a different country. That is not a data-entry slip — 25g on a 245g shoe is 10%. Plausible explanations include different colourway batches, different regional versions, or one side carrying over last generation's figure. I have no second official document that settles it, so the contradiction goes in as it stands: treat a quoted gram figure as a reference, not a specification promise.

There is a second case of the same kind. The original plan for this article treated "the Wave Medal 8 switched to an ENERZY NXT midsole" as the 7-to-8 generational change. Four Steps Instead of a Brand Shortlist

Step one: use the model code to locate the line. When a shoe looks right, confirm its prefix or five-digit code on the brand page. Do not look for the answer in the Amazon title — those titles are brand, category and three marketing claims stacked together, with no width information anywhere.

Step two: fix the width position before the length. Ask one question: does a regular last press on my instep? If no, buy a model designated 2E相当. Only if yes, look for 3E or a name containing ワイド. Do not treat 3E as an upgrade.

Step three: confirm your size exists in your chosen colour. The 93740 case shows that colour can cut the size run. The size dropdown on Amazon.co.jp only lists what the current colour carries, so a missing rung is not a bug.

Step four: confirm it is an indoor shoe. The name Resoline does not make it indoor. Look for the 室内でプレーする卓球競技専用 footnote on the brand page. If it is absent, treat the shoe as outdoor.

If you are still deciding on the blade and the rubber, those two decisions come first: the ¥10,000 blade routes, Viscaria versus Fan Zhendong ALC, and why three rubber hardness scales cannot be compared. Shoes are the last piece. If the blade and rubber are unsettled, your criteria for the shoe will drift too.

Questions

Is 2E相当 on Amazon.co.jp the same as 2E on a brand page?

相当 is a disclaimer. It means close to, without a promise of landing on a specific JIS girth figure. The Japanese trade routinely notes that the same width label varies between manufacturers, so across brands you can only compare the qualitative position — standard last or wide last — not the number.

Why is the Amazon.co.jp price so far from the brand price?

In one batch I saw both US$27 and ¥9,876, because the currency follows your exit location. Amazon.co.jp's quote to an overseas visitor is not the domestic Japanese price, and once exchange conversion is applied the absolute numbers are not comparable. Use the manufacturer's suggested retail price in yen as the baseline.

My child's foot is 22cm. Junior or adult?

22.0 is the top of the Noah Junior run and 22.5 is the bottom of the adult Noah run, so they meet. The difference is closure: the junior is elastic cord plus strap, the adult is laced. If the child cannot tie laces yet, take the junior. If they can, the adult 22.5 works.

Closing

What makes a size table annoying is that it looks like a string of numbers you can compare directly, when every number hangs on a premise. 2E is standard, not narrow. 3E is wider, not better. The same model code can change size run with a colour. The same family name can hold one indoor shoe and one outdoor shoe.

Hold those four premises and 22.5〜28.0cm（12サイズ）stops being a line of text and becomes a coordinate map you can match against your own foot. With shoes, reading the table beats paying more.

FAQ

Q: Does the Amazon Basics set have a smell when new?

A: Yes, there's typically a mild silicone odor. Wash 2-3 times before first use, or soak in warm water with a splash of white vinegar for 30 minutes — it clears up.

Q: Wood handles vs. all-silicone — which is better?

A: Wood handles are better for heat insulation and grip comfort, but can crack if left in water. All-silicone is lower maintenance and usually cheaper, but the handle feel is slippery for some users. For most people, wood is the better everyday choice.

Q: Are these dishwasher safe?

A: Most silicone utensils are top-rack dishwasher safe. However, hand washing wood handles significantly extends their lifespan. Check the specific product page for your set.

Q: How does the 14-piece Amazon Basics compare to buying 5 pieces from OXO?

A: If you need the full range (spatulas, ladles, serving spoons, slotted spoon), the Amazon Basics set wins on value. If you only need 2-3 specific tools and prioritize build quality, the smaller OXO set may work out cheaper per piece.

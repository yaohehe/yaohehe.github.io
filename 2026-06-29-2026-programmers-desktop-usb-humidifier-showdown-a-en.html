📌 Why Programmers Need a Desktop USB Humidifier (Not a Dehumidifier, Not an Air Purifier)

Let me clarify a concept that most Amazon affiliate reviews mix up: programmers' desk humidity problems fall into two opposite categories, and buying the wrong device wastes money.

  • **梅雨季 / post-monsoon (华南 Apr-Aug, 华东 Jun-Jul)** in mainland China, the Southeast Asian tropics, or anywhere humidity spikes to 75-90% — the correct device is a **dehumidifier** (covered in the 06-25 showdown — Amazon Basics B07XKGLMR8 or Pro Breeze B01HXVUT7C), not a humidifier.
  • **Beijing-style AC rooms, Shenzhen Nanshan / Shanghai Lujiazui offices with full-day central AC, and Northern Hemisphere heating season (Nov to April)**, indoor relative humidity drops to **25-35%**. At this humidity:

- Static charge accumulates on ABS / PBT keycaps; you feel a small zap on fingers when leaving the keyboard

- Air refraction under monitor light bars becomes uneven; long screen sessions cause eye strain

- Solid wood desktops (IKEA Bekant, Herman Miller abacus) lose 0.3-0.5% volume from wood grain contraction, gapping visibly within 6 months

So a humidifier is not "the opposite of a dehumidifier for humid areas" — it fixes localized comfort when room relative humidity is too low, especially for programmers doing long screen + long keystroke + long skin-dry cycles.

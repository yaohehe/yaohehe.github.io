Why Programmers Need a Phone Stand on Their Desk

You might think "just lay the phone on the desk," but in practice, that creates several problems. First, a flat phone forces you to look down at it, which is actually worse for your neck than looking at a monitor — programmers tilt their heads down 50+ times a day, and that adds up to real cervical spine stress. Second, a flat phone dissipates heat poorly, especially during MagSafe charging, where temperatures can spike and charging speeds drop by half. Third, desk space is already tight with a monitor, keyboard, and mouse taking up most of the real estate; a phone lying flat just adds to the clutter.

A good phone stand elevates the phone into your peripheral vision, so you can glance at notifications without turning your head. The upright position also exposes more surface area to air, dropping MagSafe charging temperatures by 5-8°C in my tests. And importantly, a stand takes up less desk space than a phone lying flat — it's a net win for your desk layout.

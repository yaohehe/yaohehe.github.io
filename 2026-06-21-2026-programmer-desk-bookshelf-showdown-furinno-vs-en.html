Why Programmers Need a Real Bookshelf

Three conflicts dominate every programmer's desk: the monitor riser eats half the surface, technical books multiply faster than you read them, and peripherals have nowhere to live. Stacking books on the desk works for about a week — then it becomes a mess.

A proper bookshelf solves three real problems:

  • Vertical space: Floor space is expensive; build up, not out
  • Categorization: Clean Code doesn't go next to The Mythical Man-Month; keep frequent reads at arm's reach
  • Desk surface: Your keyboard, mouse, and desk clock shouldn't compete with books for real estate

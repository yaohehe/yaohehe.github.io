Why Get a Desk Mat?

The core value is desk protection. Programmers type and mouse all day — metal keyboard edges leave permanent scratches on bare desks. Coffee cups leave rings. One desk mat ($10-30) extends your desk's life by years.

The secondary benefit is feel. A smooth PU leather or natural cork surface makes mouse tracking more precise and keystrokes more comfortable. For hours-long coding sessions, that tactile difference matters.

FAQ

Q: Will desk mats have a chemical smell?

A: Major brands like Aothia typically have no strong odor. Unbox and flatten for 12 hours before use. If it smells strongly of plastic, return it.

Q: Do 47" mats curl at the edges?

A: All long mats arrive rolled and need 1-2 days flat. Avoid placing hot cups directly on edges — this reduces edge lift significantly.

Q: How do I clean the cork side?

A: Damp cloth only, then dry immediately. Never soak cork or use abrasive cleaners.

Q: Are desk mats good for mechanical keyboards?

A: Yes. Mats provide a smoother surface than bare desks — keyboard base won't scratch and keystrokes feel more stable.
